IMPORTANT

Using the results

Species adaptation varies among varieties, locations, management systems, and years. The results are intended as a decision-support starting point rather than a substitute for local agronomic recommendations or field trials.

Soil testing, water availability, winter conditions, establishment method, intended harvest or grazing system, and local variety performance should all be considered before selecting a forage species.
