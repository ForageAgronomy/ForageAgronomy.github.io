CALCULATION METHOD

How the calculator works

The calculator uses the target Pure Live Seed (PLS) density, the species proportion in the mixture, species-specific seed weight, and seedlot quality to estimate the amount of bulk seed required.

PLS = Purity × Germination

Seeds/lb = 453,592 ÷ 1,000-seed weight (g)

Species seeding rate = Target seeds/ft² × Species proportion × 43,560 ÷ Seeds/lb ÷ PLS

The calculated value is an estimate intended to support forage establishment decisions. Seed quality, seedbed conditions, seeding equipment, weather, and local management conditions should also be considered.
