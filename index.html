OUR LAB

Understanding forage crops from field to molecule

Our research integrates forage agronomy, plant physiology, genetics, molecular biology, and breeding to understand how forage crops respond to environmental challenges and to develop productive and resilient forage systems for the western United States.

RESEARCH

Research Areas

Our research program connects fundamental plant biology with practical forage production and breeding.

🌱

Forage Agronomy

Developing agronomic practices that improve forage establishment, productivity, persistence, seed production, and quality.

  • Stand establishment
  • Nutrient management
  • Seed production
  • Winter survival
  • Forage yield and quality
🔬

Stress Physiology

Understanding physiological and molecular mechanisms underlying forage crop responses to environmental stress.

  • Salinity
  • Drought
  • Temperature stress
  • Nutrient stress
  • Root and shoot responses
🧬

Breeding & Genetics

Exploring genetic diversity and biological mechanisms associated with adaptation, resilience, and forage productivity.

  • Genetic diversity
  • Germplasm evaluation
  • Stress tolerance
  • Transcriptomics
  • Molecular markers
🌾

Sustainable Forage Systems

Developing productive, resilient, and economically viable forage systems for semiarid environments.

  • Forage–livestock systems
  • Resource-use efficiency
  • Semiarid agriculture
  • Environmental sustainability
