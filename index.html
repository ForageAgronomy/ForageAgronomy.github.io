ABOUT US

Researching forage systems for the West

The Forage Agronomy Lab at the University of Wyoming conducts research focused on improving forage production, quality, persistence, and resilience in challenging environments.

Our program brings together forage agronomy, plant physiology, forage breeding, and environmental stress research. We use field experiments, controlled-environment studies, physiological measurements, and molecular approaches to understand how forage plants respond to their environments.

Our work is designed to address the needs of Wyoming and the broader western United States, while contributing to a broader understanding of plant–environment interactions.

OUR MISSION

Understanding plants. Improving forage systems.

Our mission is to develop science-based solutions that improve the productivity, quality, persistence, and resilience of forage systems while advancing our understanding of plant responses to environmental challenges.

Through integrated research, extension, and education, we aim to translate plant science into practical knowledge that benefits producers, agricultural professionals, students, and the forage and livestock industries.

OUR WORK

From the field to the laboratory

Our work connects field research, controlled environments, plant physiology, and forage management to address challenges facing forage production in Wyoming and the western U.S.
