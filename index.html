About the Lab

Research for resilient forage production

The Forage Agronomy Lab at the University of Wyoming conducts research focused on improving the productivity, quality, persistence, and sustainability of forage crops and forage-based systems.

Our research integrates forage agronomy, crop physiology, plant–environment interactions, and field-based experimentation to address challenges affecting forage production in Wyoming and the broader western United States.

We work across field and controlled-environment settings to understand how forage crops respond to environmental conditions and management practices and to develop practical approaches that can benefit producers.

Research Interests

Understanding forage crops in changing environments

Our research addresses biological and agronomic factors that influence forage production, quality, persistence, and resilience.

Forage Crop Productivity

Agronomic practices that improve forage yield, quality, establishment, and persistence.

Environmental Stress

Plant responses to environmental conditions that constrain forage production and persistence.

Water & Nutrient Management

Understanding how water and nutrient availability influence forage crop performance.

Perennial Forage Systems

Improving the productivity and long-term persistence of perennial forage crops and mixtures.
