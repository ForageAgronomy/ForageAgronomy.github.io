OUR LAB

Research for resilient forage agriculture

The Forage Agronomy Lab at the University of Wyoming conducts applied research to improve the productivity, quality, persistence, and resilience of forage crops and forage-based systems.

We investigate how forage crops respond to environmental conditions and management practices and translate research findings into practical strategies for forage producers and agricultural systems.
