OUR LAB

Understanding forage crops from field to molecule

Our research integrates forage agronomy, plant physiology, genetics, molecular biology, and breeding to understand how forage crops respond to environmental challenges and to develop productive and resilient forage systems.

RESEARCH

Research Areas

Our research program connects fundamental plant biology with practical forage production and breeding.

01

Forage Agronomy

Developing agronomic practices that improve forage establishment, productivity, persistence, seed production, and quality.

  • Stand establishment
  • Nutrient management
  • Forage yield and quality
02

Forage Seed Production

Optimizing agronomic management and physiological mechanisms to maximize seed yield, establishment efficiency, and reproductive potential in forage seed production systems.

  • Lodging management
  • Nutrient management
  • Seed yield components
03

Breeding & Genetics

Exploring genetic diversity and biological mechanisms associated with adaptation, resilience, and forage productivity.

  • Germplasm evaluation
  • Omics
  • Abiotic stress tolerance
04

Sustainable Forage Systems

Developing productive, resilient, and economically viable forage systems for semiarid environments.

  • Forage–livestock systems
  • Resource-use efficiency
  • Ecosystem services
