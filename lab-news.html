August 2026 Conference

Kapil Khadka Presents at the 2026 Tear Down the Walls Meeting

On August 18, 2026, Kapil Khadka presented his research at the 2026 Tear Down the Walls Meeting held at the University of Wyoming. His presentation was titled Effects of Osmopriming and Hydropriming on Seed Germination of Alfalfa under Neutral and Alkaline Salt Stress. The work contributes to the lab's broader research on improving alfalfa establishment under challenging soil conditions.

May 2026 New Member

Dr. Grace Bagunu Vinarao Joins as Postdoctoral Research Associate

We are pleased to welcome Dr. Grace Bagunu Vinarao to the Forage Agronomy Lab as a Postdoctoral Research Associate, co-supervised by Dr. Donna Harris. Dr. Vinarao completed her Ph.D. in Plant Science at the University of Wyoming and brings strong expertise in forage breeding and genetics. She is based at the Sheridan Research and Extension Center, where she will conduct her research as part of our forage breeding and genetics program.

January 2026 New Member

Heidi Pick Joins as M.S. Student

Heidi Pick has joined the Forage Agronomy Lab as an M.S. student, co-supervised by Dr. Andrea Garfinkel. Heidi's research will focus on forage seed production. She is based at the Powell Research and Extension Center, where she will conduct her field and applied research.

January 2026 New Member

Kapil Khadka Joins as M.S. Student

Kapil Khadka has joined the Forage Agronomy Lab as an M.S. student. His research will focus on alfalfa physiology and management, contributing to the lab's ongoing efforts to improve forage productivity and persistence under diverse production environments.

January 2025 Announcement

Dr. Surendra Bhattarai joins the University of Wyoming

Dr. Surendra Bhattarai joined the Department of Plant Sciences at the University of Wyoming in January 2025 as Assistant Professor of Forage Agronomy. Prior to this appointment, he served as a Research Scientist at SARDA Ag Research in Alberta, Canada, where he contributed to applied forage research programs in the region.

Dr. Bhattarai completed his M.S., Ph.D., and Postdoctoral training at the University of Saskatchewan, where his work focused on forage crop physiology, breeding and genomics. He brings a strong foundation in both fundamental and applied forage science to the University of Wyoming and its land-grant mission.
